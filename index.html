What the scores mean
  • Form checks path placement against the reference shape.
  • Order checks whether strokes were made in the reference sequence.
  • Direction checks whether each stroke travels the same way.
  • Pressure checks where the stroke gets heavier or lighter. Pressure confidence says whether the device data looks real, flat, or missing.
  • Rhythm checks whether the movement is steady or hesitant.

Author mode
In Author mode, your current strokes replace the reference strokes when you click “Adopt attempt as reference”. Pressure and order are preserved.
